3. Portfolio Narrative

Week 39 was a capital-structure week more than an operating week. Physical occupancy held or improved at most reporting assets - Skye Isle (93.5%), Skye Ridge (94.0%), Skye at Love (91.1%, up from 89.8%) and Park at Peachtree Hills (96.6%) all moved the right way, and collections improved at Upland (80% to 84%) and Skye Ridge (88.1% to 90.8%). The unit-weighted occupancy of the 11 assets that reported a figure is 87.8%, dragged down almost entirely by Skye Reserve's 982 units at 77.3%.

The binding constraints are debt and liquidity. Skye Reserve's ~$200M loan matures in April with MF1 asking for a $60M paydown and ~$450K of rate-cap arrears and extension cost outstanding. Skye at Conway is worth 5-7% less than its loan and Starwood rejected ZMR's $750K offer. Chimney Hill is carrying ~$1.48M of AP on ~$285K of cash with August books still open. Across the RPM portfolio, more than $650K is owed to the manager because waterfalls pay debt service and escrows first, and Skye at Love, Skye Isle and Skye Ridge are waiting on lender or equity funding to clear payables.

The team is responding on the right levers: stricter delinquency rules and TransUnion screening at Upland, a funded renovation test at Skye Ridge where renovated units command $236-$270 premiums, lender extension requests on the Newmark repairs, and AM fees moving above the line in the 2027 budgets. Budget season is the other major workstream - first solid PMC drafts are due Oct 9, and reviews at Preserve and Hunter's Creek surfaced loss-to-lease, concession and fee assumptions that were materially off T12 actuals.

Coverage remains uneven. Nightingale was not discussed, and Ninety-Nine 44, both Skye Oaks parcels, Boardwalk and Hanley Place produced no discrete occupancy or collections figures. Preserve at Riverwalk got its first operating read in weeks (~92% occupied), attributed from a site call whose summary did not name the property.
