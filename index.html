Portfolio Narrative

Week 43 was dominated by liquidity and asset-condition issues rather than leasing momentum. Skye Reserve remains the portfolio's largest exposure: fire-watch costs were cut sharply, but cracked structural elements, mold and roughly $2M of deferred work keep lender oversight high. Hanley Place and Boardwalk payables are a second pressure point, while the Walnut assets in Dallas (Parks and Ninety-Nine 44) are stuck near 84% occupancy and Upland's collections slipped to 83% with a possible ~$100K licensing cost. On the positive side, Hunter's Creek, Conway, Skye Isle and Skye Ridge are performing, Isle and Love secured a maturity extension to June 2027, and the Houston and Genoa acquisitions moved forward. A Yardi-to-Cemetery feed outage means collections data has been missing since October 17, so collections figures this week are partial.

Top 5 Risks

  • 1. Skye Reserve
    Fire-system, mold and structural issues at a 982-unit asset: ~$200K-$300K fire work plus ~$2M deferred items, cracked beam and closed staircase, mold remediation (~130 units, $650K-$750K est.). Lender oversight is heightened on ~$200M MF1/CBRE debt; recovery needs 90-92% occupancy versus 79.02% leased today.
    Mitigation: Fire watch cut from six staff to one (savings ~$4K/day) but deficiencies remain open. Get the structural engineer assessment and a lender-approved remediation plan.
  • 2. Hanley Place / The Boardwalk
    Hanley Place aging AP exceeds $500K and may approach $1M with lien and invoice-practice concerns; Boardwalk holds ~$222K capital against ~$232K AP. Cash sources are thin (Hanley ~$76K insurance surplus).
    Mitigation: Audit AP aging and liens at both assets; confirm funding sources before Friday lender budget submissions.
  • 3. Upland Townhomes
    Collections slid from ~88% to 83% (prior-issue residents are ~$36K of delinquency) while licensing may require two backflow devices (~$100K), 25% unit inspections and possible six-figure repairs.
    Mitigation: Accelerate set-outs, finalize licensing strategy with the city, and obtain backflow and electrical bids.
  • 4. Parks At Walnut / Ninety-Nine 44
    Both Dallas Walnut assets sit near 84% occupancy with collections of 88-90%, soft Texas fundamentals, and a possible property-tax funding need; investor (Watershed) scrutiny of historical reserve and capex use is rising.
    Mitigation: Hold concession plan, confirm tax escrow and ACRE funding (~$150K vs $360K), and complete the capital reconciliation for one property first.
  • 5. Capital structure: Isle/Love, Skye Ridge, Conway
    Isle and Love maturity extended to June 2027 but additional funding is disfavored; Skye Ridge partners could not fund the $360K call; Conway's Jan 9 loan maturity needs a sale, extension or recap decision. A Yardi-to-Cemetery feed outage (collections missing since 10/17) also limits reporting visibility.
    Mitigation: Decide funding stance with Atlantic Creek and Rialto this week; finish Conway model; restore the data feed.
