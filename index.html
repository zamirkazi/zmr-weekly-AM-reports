Portfolio Narrative

Week 40 was defined by cash, not leasing. Most assets that reported held or improved occupancy - the 3,984 units with a stated figure averaged 88.4% (vs 87.8% last week), and the eight assets reporting collections averaged 95.6% (vs 89.3%), with Hunter's Creek, Skye Isle, Conway, Skye at Love, Chimney Hill and Peachtree all at or above 97%. The pressure is on the balance sheet. Hanley Place has ~$856K of payables against ~$229K of cash with vendors suspending service; Skye Ridge's August expenses exceeded NOI, leaving a debt-service shortfall and $366K of AP while a $400K capital call is still being validated; Boardwalk has vendor service cutoffs; and Preserve is clearing $252K of AP. Megan and RPM are moving the eight cash-managed properties to monthly cash-flow and weekly AP reviews, and Q4 controls will target late and unsupported invoices.

Skye Reserve (77.7%, 982 units) remains the single largest exposure: leasing is moving toward 80%, but 18 move-in units are not system-ready, three maintenance/housekeeping roles are open, and the financing path (cash-flow mortgage, ~$10M MF1 piece, IHG raise) is unresolved with ~$650K of fees outstanding. On the capital side, Conway's maturity is approaching against a ~$33-35M BOV, Starwood rejected the latest funding proposal, and the 10-year Treasury near 5.2% is compressing proceeds.

Organizationally, Nicole Chang has left ZMR. Megan is covering in the interim and a reallocation of Nicole's eleven properties is under discussion but not decided. This report keeps the Section 10 asset-manager mapping as required, with the transition flagged. 2027 budget reviews continued across the portfolio, with recurring issues in rent-growth annualization, concessions math and chart-of-accounts mapping; first solid drafts remain targeted for mid-to-late October.
