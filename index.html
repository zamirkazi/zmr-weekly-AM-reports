Portfolio Narrative

Portfolio occupancy was 88.4% (unit-weighted across 13 properties and 4,009 reporting units), flat against 88.4% in Week 40. Skye Reserve (78.4%, 982 units) still pulls the average down, while Conway, Peachtree and Skye Isle are at 95%. Average reported collections were 90.4% across 12 properties, but the range was wide: Skye Isle reported 99.5% while Upland's October month-to-date figure was 74%. Status count: 2 green, 10 amber, 7 red.

Liquidity and debt events drove the week. The Julia has a December 29 trustee sale on the calendar, and the lender has said it prefers to take control. Skye at Conway's January 9 maturity and its missed rate-cap deadline need a decision. Payables are still the main operating stress: Hanley Place is near $800K, Skye Reserve $975K and Skye Ridge $408K, and vendor liens are surfacing at Conway and Skye Ridge. On the positive side, about $695K in Florida tax and insurance over-accruals may come back from lender escrows.

On the ground, Q4 leasing is slowing seasonally. Concessions are being pulled back at Upland and Hanley and extended at The Boardwalk, 9944 and Chimney Hill. The Isle/Love collections-bonus model is the clearest win and is being considered for other Texas assets. Internally, 2027 budgets are targeted for Oct 23 and QARs for Oct 22/29. Since Nicole's departure, analyst capacity is limited and one deadline has already been missed.
